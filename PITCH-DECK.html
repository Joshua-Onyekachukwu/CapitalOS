The Problem

Raising capital is broken

Founders spend 40-60% of their time on fundraising logistics instead of building their companies.
  • Fragmented tools. Spreadsheets, email, Google Docs, CRM, LinkedIn — none of them talk to each other.
  • Manual research. Finding the right investors means hours scrolling through Crunchbase, AngelList, and LinkedIn.
  • Generic outreach. Most founders send the same mass email to 200 investors. Investors can tell.
  • No intelligence. No system tells a founder "these 12 investors are the best match for your company."
  • No system. Fundraising is a process, not a single event. But founders have no unified way to manage it.

The Solution

The operating system for raising capital

One intelligent platform that replaces the entire fragmented fundraising stack.
  • Understands your company. Tell us about your business once. Capital OS remembers and uses that intelligence across everything.
  • Discovers the right investors. 46,000+ verified investors. AI scores and ranks every investor against your specific company profile.
  • Qualifies every opportunity. Multi-dimensional fit scoring: stage, sector, geography, check size, thesis alignment, portfolio overlap.
  • Generates personalized outreach. AI writes emails that sound like you wrote them. Each one is specific to the investor and their thesis.
  • Manages the full pipeline. From first discovery to meeting to close. Track every investor through every stage. Never lose momentum.
