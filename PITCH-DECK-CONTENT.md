## 2. The Problem

Raising capital is broken for startup founders.

**What founders deal with today:**

- **Fragmented tools.** Spreadsheets, email, Google Docs, pitch-deck templates, CRM tools, and LinkedIn — none of them talk to each other.
- **Hours of manual research.** Finding the right investors means scrolling through Crunchbase, AngelList, and LinkedIn, trying to figure out who actually invests in your space.
- **Generic outreach.** Most founders send the same mass email to 200 investors. It doesn't work. Investors can tell.
- **No intelligence.** There is no system that tells a founder "these 12 investors are the best match for your company, here's why, and here's what to say to each one."
- **No system.** Fundraising is a process, not a single event. But founders have no unified way to manage discovery, qualification, outreach, follow-ups, and tracking.

**The result:** Founders spend 40-60% of their time on fundraising activities that could be automated. They miss the right investors. They send bad emails. They lose momentum.

---

## 3. The Solution

**Capital OS is the operating system for raising capital.**

It replaces the entire fragmented fundraising stack with one intelligent platform that:

1. **Understands your company** — You tell us about your business once. Capital OS remembers and uses that intelligence across everything.

2. **Discovers the right investors** — We maintain a database of 46,000+ verified investors. Our AI scores and ranks every investor against your specific company profile.

3. **Qualifies every opportunity** — Multi-dimensional fit scoring considers stage, sector, geography, check size, thesis alignment, and portfolio overlap. You see a score and the reasoning behind it.

4. **Generates personalized outreach** — AI writes emails that sound like you wrote them. Not generic templates. Each email is specific to the investor, their thesis, their portfolio, and why your company fits.

5. **Manages the full pipeline** — From first discovery to meeting to close. Track every investor through every stage. Never lose momentum.

6. **Handles campaigns** — Organize investors into campaigns, set up follow-up sequences, and let the system manage timing, frequency, and stop-on-reply rules.

---

## 4. How It Works

**Step 1: Tell us about your company**
7-step onboarding. Industry, stage, funding amount, traction, team, documents. Takes 5 minutes.

**Step 2: We find your investors**
AI-powered discovery across 46,000+ investors. Filtered by stage, sector, geography, check size. Every result is scored.

**Step 3: You review and qualify**
See fit scores with explanations. Generate AI research summaries on any investor. Understand who they are before you reach out.

**Step 4: We draft your emails**
AI generates personalized outreach for each investor. Different tone for each one. You review, edit, approve, send.

**Step 5: We manage follow-ups**
Built-in email sequences. Follow-up scheduling. Stop-on-reply. Business hours only. Never spam an investor.

**Step 6: You track everything**
Pipeline view. Campaign metrics. Open rates. Click rates. Reply rates. Know exactly where every investor stands.
