## Slide 1 — The Problem

Raising capital is broken for startup founders.

## Slide 2 — Why a monthly SaaS doesn't work for fundraising

This is the slide most fundraising tools miss.

- A funding round is rarely a one-month event. It is a 2–6 month grind of outreach, follow-ups, meetings, and re-engagement.
- If you charge $49/month and the round drags, the founder cancels — whether they succeed or fail. That is the fast-cancel trap.
- After the round closes, founders are still expected to update their new investors every month. There is no tool built for that — they go back to Gmail and spreadsheets.

**Takeaway:** fundraising is a lifecycle, not a feature list. Pricing should match the shape of a raise.

## Slide 8 — How it works (end to end)

**Phase A — Before the Sprint: get the company into the system**

1. Tell us about your company — 7-step onboarding. Industry, stage, funding, traction, team, docs. ~5 minutes. The company profile feeds the AI across everything.
2. We find your investors — AI-powered discovery across the investor database, filtered by stage, sector, geography, check size. Every result scored with explanations.

**Phase B — The Sprint: run the raise**

3. Review and qualify — fit scores with per-factor explanations. Generate AI research summaries on any investor.
4. We draft your emails — AI generates personalised outreach per investor. Different tone per investor. You review, edit, approve, send.
5. We manage follow-ups — built-in sequences, scheduling, stop-on-reply, business hours only. Never spam an investor.
6. Track everything — pipeline view, campaign metrics, open rates, click rates, reply rates.

**Phase C — After the Sprint: keep the relationships warm**

7. The Sprint ends — if the round closed, the platform offers Post-Raise Retention.
8. Monthly updates run on autopilot — metrics flow in, the update draft is generated, founder reviews and sends. Read receipts and click tracking tell the founder who is engaged.
9. The data room stays current — board decks, cap table exports, past updates stored and ready.
10. The next round is easier — 18–24 months later, current backers are warm, organised, reachable with one click.

## Slide 10 — The investor database: what we have today

The investor database is the platform's core data asset. Here is its actual state — honest numbers, not aspirational ones.

**Active investors in database:** ~19,000 (US/UK/EU focus)

**Investor sources (multi-source pipeline):**

| Source | Investors | Status |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| SEC EDGAR 13F-HR (fund names) | ~26,000 raw → deduped to active set | Imported |
| SEC EDGAR Form D | ~5,000 | Imported |
| SEC EDGAR N-CEN | ~2,000 | Imported |
| FishTank VC profiles | ~18,000 | Imported |
| Apollo enrichment | ~25,000 raw → deduped to active set | Imported |
| Web scraping | ~7,000 | Imported |

**Contact data coverage — the gap we are actively closing:**

This is the honest part of the pitch. The database has strong firm-level intelligence: stages, sectors, geographies, check sizes, types. What it has lacked is contact-level data — the actual email addresses and LinkedIn profiles that make outreach possible.

We have been fixing this. A free SMTP reachability probe (MX + RCPT TO checks, no per-email cost) ran across ~4,900 investor email candidates and found:
- ~2.5% of probed addresses exist and are deliverable
- ~44% hard-rejected at SMTP RCPT stage
- ~24% had no MX record at all
- The rest timed out or were unknown

The probe writes `smtp-rcpt` provenance rows into the database, and the re-qualified scoring engine reads that evidence — so the investors we can actually reach get properly scored, and the ones we cannot are flagged instead of silently sending.

**Takeaway:** we are a platform with a strong data foundation and a clear, execution-focused plan to convert that foundation into contactable investor records. The pipeline exists. The verification tooling exists. The re-scoring engine exists. We are mid-execution on the data, not starting from zero.

**Realistic estimate:** 200,000–300,000 investors with the current approach. To reach 1M+ we need LinkedIn Sales Navigator access, Crunchbase Pro, or PitchBook API.

**Data quality architecture:**
- Deterministic scoring, not AI-hallucinated fit. 8-factor model, rules-based, explainable. Every score has a written reason per factor.
- Contactability as a hard gate. Unverified deliverable email cannot make an investor outreach-ready — the readiness gate checks verified-deliverable email status, not just the presence of an address.
- Provenance tracking. Every data point has a source: provider, web research, firm website, manual entry, AI-inferred, or public records.
- Source prioritisation. SEC-filed contacts rank above directory contacts, which rank above inferred addresses.
