# Investor Market Research — Capital OS

**Date:** October 24, 2026 · Methodology: current web sources (2024–2026) cited inline; where precise counts cannot be verified, ranges and confidence are given and labeled. Compiled from three web-research passes focused on (A) Nigeria/Africa, (B) US/UK/Europe online investor market, (C) competitor pricing and complaints (details in `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md`).

**Positioning note:** Capital OS is a **founder-side** fundraising tool. The "investor market" matters to Capital OS in two different ways, kept separate throughout:

1. **Supply side** — the universe of investors that populate the database and validate fit-scoring (the data product).
2. **Buyer side** — whether investors themselves are ever an customer segment (they are **not** the initial target: investor-side CRMs are a different, enterprise-priced category).

### How Nigerian/African deal discovery actually happens (observed, not assumed from folklore)

- Angel-network-sourced deal flow with group diligence (LAN DealDays; quarterly investor meetups documented by [Orange Corners](https://www.orangecorners.com/photos-from-orange-corners-nigeria-lagos-angel-network-investor-meetup/)).
- Marketplace-style online matching (Nigeria Angel Investment Network's pitch-then-email workflow is documented on its own site).
- Nearly half of surveyed African angels invest via syndicates ([African Business, 5 Nov 2025](https://african.business/2025/11/innov-africa-deals/africas-angel-investors-power-a-new-wave-of-startup-growth)).
- Claims like "investors use WhatsApp/spreadsheets to track deals" surfaced **no solid national evidence** and are treated as hypotheses to validate in discovery interviews, not as facts.

## C. Quantification — the honest funnel

Converting anchors into an initial addressable-and-reachable figure (method: subtract the segments that already have dedicated software or a funding platform out of the population; label every step as an estimate):

1. **Total relevant investor organizations (global, all segments)**: 200 (US ACA orgs) + 160 (UKBAA orgs) + 359 (EU networks) + **110 (African angel networks)** + **~3,417 US VC firms / ~4,900 active global VC firms** + ~50 regional accelerators-of-majority → **order 10,000–11,500 organizations** (high estimate; overlaps exist).
2. **Total individuals across those structures** — successfully *est.* **5,000 (Africa networks) to 445,000 (US active angels)** depending on whether "anyone who wrote an angel check last year" or "online platform Participants" is the frame. **Use 30,000–60,000 as the working population of "online-market participating investors"** (US/UK/EU platform users, commonly cited across OpenVC/Gust/Signal/Wellfound).
3. **Nigeria-specific investors reachable** — 173 round participants (2024) + network members (LAN ~110 self-report) + diaspora subset of ABAN's respondents → realistic **300–800** active founding-side-facing investors in Nigeria.
4. **Potential paying customers (Capital OS = founder tool)** — the buyer is a *founder*, not the investor. Global annual count of ~55,000 ventures receiving angel investment (US CVR alone) plus hundreds of thousands of founders *attempting* fundraising is the funnel ceiling; **a realistic 12-month obtainable target at Nigerian/African launch prices = 100–400 paying founder accounts** (see pricing experiments in Product Roadmap; figures are estimates, not benchmarks).
5. **Reachable initial segment (Nigeria-first)** — **Nigerian founders raising pre-seed/seed from (a) 110–800 local/diaspora angels and (b) the ~24 African-US VC funds active in Nigeria deals**. Identifiers: Lagos/Abuja startup communities, YCombinator/Angelist Africa public lists, accelerators (~150), and the founder networks the founder-operator already belongs to. Confidence: **medium-high that the segment is reachable cheaply; medium that they pay** — because no evidence exists in either direction about willingness-to-pay in this niche; that's what the pilot pricing experiments decide.

## D. Open risks to these figures

- AVCA/Partech/Briter use different definitions (VC vs equity vs tech; active vs membership). Overlaps mean the global estimate is a *frame*, not a sum.
- Nigeria范围内的 diaspora investor counts and family offices: unverifiable with public data; flagged rather than invented.
- ABAN "5,000+ / 33% diaspora" is a survey; audience of_ used only as a share, never multiplied faithfully into a count.
- Platform display counts (398K "investors") are marketing figures. **Assume scraped directory entries are not verified active investors — which is exactly what Capital OS's own verification/status fields render for the user.**
