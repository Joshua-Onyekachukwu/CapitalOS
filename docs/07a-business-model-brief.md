# Business Model Brief — Capital OS

> A one-page version of the model, for emails, intros, and quick investor conversations. Longer version: `docs/07-pricing-billing.md`, `CAPITAL-OS-INVESTOR-PITCH.md`.

---

## The model in one paragraph

Capital OS is priced around the actual shape of a raise, not around generic SaaS feature lists.

1. **Upfront Fundraise Sprint** — $499 (3-month) or $999 (6-month Pro), paid upfront. Everything the platform does for an actively raising founder: investor database, AI thesis matching, fit scoring, AI research, personalized email drafting, 7 branded templates with open/click tracking and CAN-SPAM, drip sequences with stop-on-reply, 11-stage pipeline Kanban, email health dashboard, pitch deck generator, and AI Copilot.
2. **Post-Raise Retention** — $29/month or $290/year. After the round closes, automated monthly update drafts pulled from the founder's real metrics, read-receipt and click tracking, a continuous secure data room, and warm-backer re-engagement for the next round.
3. **B2B Multi-Client** — $499–$1,000/month, billed annually. Accelerators, studios, and fractional CFOs managing batches of startups get a multi-company dashboard, shared pipeline, firm intelligence seats, and per-startup billing visibility.

We do not charge a monthly fee that founders cancel when the round drags; we do not take a percentage of money raised. Both choices are deliberate.

---

## Why this model

- Fundraising is episodic. A round takes 3–6 months. A $49/month SaaS gets cancelled whether the raise succeeds or fails. A $499 upfront Sprint secures LTV on day one and aligns incentives with the founder's outcome.
- After close, the founder still has to update their new investors every month. A $29/month retention tier turns a one-time customer into a years-long relationship, at negligible marginal cost.
- Accelerators and advisors manage 10–50 startups at once and have corporate budgets. A small base of B2B customers provides stable ARR that smooths founder seasonality.
- Taking a success fee without a registered U.S. Broker-Dealer license violates Section 15(a) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. Selling software, access passes, and usage credits keeps the business 100% compliant.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Free tier AI cost/month | ~$0.15 |
| Workspace AI cost/month (500 credits) | ~$5.00 |
| Workspace gross margin | ~90% |
| LTV — Sprint + Retention (2-year view) | $499 + $29×24 ≈ $1,195 per successful founder, plus B2B |

---

## What we do not do

- No success fee. No percentage of money raised. Clean under U.S. securities law.
- No open-ended monthly SaaS that founders cancel when the raise drags. The monthly option is the $29 retention tier for post-close founders.
