# Qualification framework

Suitability for Capital OS is "does this investor actually fund pre-seed/seed
startups that look like our founders", not "how much do we know about them".
Research completeness is capped at 5 points for that reason.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | Weight | Evidence that earns points |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Pre-seed / seed alignment | 25 | stated stage focus, or ≥2 portfolio companies funded at pre-seed/seed |
| 2 | Sector relevance | 15 | stated sector mandate, or portfolio concentration in the founder's sector |
| 3 | Geographic compatibility | 15 | invests in the founder's market, or explicitly invests internationally |
| 4 | Cheque-size compatibility | 15 | stated cheque range, or ≥2 disclosed cheques overlapping the round |
| 5 | Demonstrated early-stage activity | 10 | public deals in the last 36 months at pre-seed/seed |
| 6 | Founder / company profile fit | 10 | stated openness to first-time, solo, technical or immigrant founders |
| 7 | Activity recency | 5 | a public deal or filing in the last 18 months |
| 8 | Contact and research completeness | 5 | usable public contact channel + sourced profile |
| — | **Total** | **100** | |

## Evidence rules per dimension

1. **Stage.** A seed-round participant is not proof of pre-seed interest. Stated
   "pre-seed and seed" counts; a single seed deal does not earn the full 25.
2. **Sector.** One AI-using portfolio company does not make a generalist fund an
   AI investor. Stated thesis, or ≥3 of ≥5 recent deals in the sector.
3. **Geography.** Headquarters is not investment geography. A US fund that
   requires US incorporation is not compatible with a Nigerian company unless it
   says it invests internationally.
4. **Cheque.** Distinguish initial cheque, round participation, round size, fund
   size and follow-on capacity. Only initial-cheque evidence earns points.
   Estimated ranges are marked `basis: derived_from_deals`.
5. **Activity.** Filing recency (Form D) proves a vehicle is raising, not that it
   is deploying into startups. Deals are required.
6. **Founder fit.** Only explicit statements or repeated portfolio patterns.
7. **Recency.** Stale by 18 months unless the fund is between vintages.
8. **Completeness.** Never more than 5; cannot lift a record into a higher tier
   on its own.

## Tiers

| Tier | Score | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 85–100 | strong, current pre-seed/seed evidence with compatible cheque and geography |
| B | 70–84 | relevant, one or two dimensions thin |
| C | 50–69 | plausible, needs enrichment before outreach |
| D | < 50 | insufficient evidence or poor fit |
| Unverified | — | discovered, not yet scored (e.g. Form D candidate with no enrichment) |

## Disqualification

A record is disqualified, not scored, when it is:

- not an investor (operating company, service provider, SPV with no manager);
- a duplicate of a promoted record;
- a fund that has never invested (fund formed, nothing deployed) — keep as
  "potential", not qualified;
- outside stage scope (growth/PE-only) with no early-stage activity;
- an entity we cannot evidence at all beyond a name.

## Where the current batch sits

All 11,054 staged Form D candidates score **16/100, Tier D (unverified)**:
recency 5, geography 7.5, completeness 3.75, everything else 0. They are a
backlog to enrich, not a pipeline of qualified investors. Promotion requires
stage, sector and cheque evidence from fund sites, filings or licensed data.
